Fire season

Get your gutters fire-ready before summer.

The CFA tells people in bushfire areas to clear their gutters in early spring, before the Fire Danger Period is declared. Dry leaves in a gutter are where embers land. Book now and you'll get a dated photo report you can show the council or your insurer.

Why it matters

Blocked gutters get expensive, fast.

A full gutter can't carry water to the downpipe, so rain spills over the back edge and soaks into the house. The damage is slow, out of sight, and costs far more to fix than a clean.

Fascia and eaves

Rotting timber

Overflow runs down the fascia board behind the gutter and into the eaves. The timber stays wet, paint peels, and the boards rot until the gutter has nothing solid to hang from.

Gutters

Rust from the inside

Wet leaves hold water against the metal long after the rain stops. Steel gutters rust through from the inside out, and once they're rusted through they can't be cleaned, only replaced.

Roof, walls and footings

Water where it shouldn't be

Water backs up into the roof space, stains ceilings and runs down inside walls. Overflow at ground level soaks garden beds and the soil along the footings.

Replacing fascia and gutters means a carpenter and a licensed plumber, and a bill many times the price of a clean. A clean from ${{(index .Site.Prices.Types 0).Price}} is the cheap part.
