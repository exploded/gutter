Questions

The small print, in plain English.

Is there a call-out fee?

No. The price for your type of house is the whole price, anywhere in the service area.

What if a downpipe is blocked?

Most clear when they're flushed, which is included. If one is packed solid and needs a jetter, it's ${{$p.Downpipe}} — and only after you say yes.

What isn't included?

Repairs. Fixing or replacing gutters and downpipes is licensed plumbing work in Victoria, so if I find a problem I'll photograph it and tell you. I also don't install gutter guard at the moment.

Do you charge GST?

No GST is charged — I'm not registered for GST. The price shown is the total.

Do I need to be home?

No, as long as I can get to the side of the house. Tell me about locked gates, dogs or solar panels when you book.

What about bad weather or fire danger days?

I don't work on roofs in rain, high wind, or on Extreme and Catastrophic fire danger days. If the weather turns, I'll move you to the next good day.
