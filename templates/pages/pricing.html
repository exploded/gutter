Questions

The small print, in plain English.

Is there a call-out fee?

No. The price for your type of house is the whole price, anywhere in the service area.

What if a downpipe is blocked?

If we find one, we'll tell you. Where it can be reached, we can clear it with a jetter for ${{$p.Downpipe}} — and only after you say yes. Some downpipes, especially on taller houses, can't be reached, so flushing isn't part of the standard clean.

What isn't included?

Repairs. Fixing or replacing gutters and downpipes is licensed plumbing work in Victoria, so if we find a problem, we'll photograph it and tell you.

Do you charge GST?

No GST is charged — the business isn't registered for GST. The price shown is the total.

Do I need to be home?

No, as long as we can get to the side of the house. Tell us about locked gates, dogs or solar panels when you book.

What about bad weather or fire danger days?

We don't work in rain, high wind, or on Extreme and Catastrophic fire danger days. If the weather turns, we'll move you to the next good day.
