On the day

  1. Ask while you show them the photos, not as you drive off — that's when they're most pleased.
  2. Hand them the card. Their phone camera scans it straight through to the review form.
  3. Ask everyone, not just the happy ones, and never offer a discount for a review — Google's rules ban both.
  4. Phone instead? Show this page on your phone — their camera scans it straight off the screen.
  5. Not signed in to Google? Don't fight it. Say you'll send a link with the receipt, then tick Ask for a Google review when you mark the invoice paid.
